

All answers below are taken verbatim from the page content at shohoz.com/bus-tickets:

**1. Helpline, hours, email**
- Helpline: **16374** (shown as "1 6 3 7 4")
- Operating hours: **7:00 AM to 11:00 PM** (header shows "7 AM - 11 PM")
- Support email: **info@shohoz.com**

**2. Cancellation & migration rules**
- Customers must have **12/24 hours before trip departure time, depending on the operator** (applies to both cancellation and migration).
- Migration requires calling helpline **16374**; the strict rule: **"the customer must call from his ticket booking number"** — i.e., the phone call must come from the same number the ticket was booked on.
- Web cancellation is allowed only with enough time remaining — **12/24 hours excluding 11 PM to 7 AM** (those night hours are explicitly excluded from the calculation window).

**3. Refund timelines**
- **bKash & Nagad:** maximum **5–7 working days**
- **Internet banking, debit & credit cards:** maximum **7–10 working days**

**4. Search bar**
- Trip types: **One Way** / **Round Way** toggle
- Fields: **From**, **To**, **Journey Date** (placeholder "Pick a date")
- Actions: **Swap Stations** button, **"+ Add Return Trip"** button, and **Search** button

**5. Operators & single account**
- Claim: **"More Than 100+ Trusted Bus Operators"** (also "100+ operators nationwide")
- Single Shohoz account covers: **Bus, Air, and Park** ("Now access Bus, Air, and Park with a single Shohoz account")
