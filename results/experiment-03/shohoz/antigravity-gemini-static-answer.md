### 2. Rules and Time Windows for Ticket Cancellation and Migration
* **General Time Window**: Customers must have **12 or 24 hours** (12/24 hours) before the trip departure time, depending on the operator.

* **Explicitly Excluded Hours from Cancellation Calculation Window**: 
  * The hours between **11:00 PM and 7:00 AM** are explicitly excluded from the cancellation calculation window (`12/24 hours excluding 11 PM to 7 AM`).
* **Strict Rule for the Phone Number Used for Migration**: 
  * The customer **must call from their ticket booking number** (*"the customer must call from his ticket booking number"*).
