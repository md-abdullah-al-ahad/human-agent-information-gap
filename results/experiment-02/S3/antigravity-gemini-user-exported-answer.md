Based strictly and solely on the content displayed on the official [Gov.uk Standard Visitor](https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor) overview page, here are the answers to your questions:

---

### 1. Standard Visa Application Fee and Maximum Permitted Duration of Stay

* **Application Fee**: A Standard Visitor visa costs **£135** for up to 6 months.
* **Standard Maximum Permitted Duration of Stay**: You can usually stay in the UK for **up to 6 months**. (The page notes: *"You might be able to apply to stay for longer in certain circumstances, for example to get medical treatment."*)

---

### 2. Activities Visitors Are Permitted to Do Under This Visa

Under the section **"What you can and cannot do (‘permitted activities’)"**, the page states:

> "You can visit the UK as a Standard Visitor:
> * for tourism, for example on a holiday or vacation
> * to see your family or friends
> * to volunteer for up to 30 days with a registered charity
> * to pass through the UK to another country (‘in transit’)
> * for certain business activities, for example attending a meeting or interview
> * for certain paid engagements or events (a ‘permitted paid engagement’) as an expert in your profession, for example to give lectures or perform
> * for certain Erasmus+ placements
> * to take part in a school exchange programme
> * to do a recreational course of up to 30 days, for example a dance course
> * to study, do a placement or take an exam
> * as an academic, senior doctor or dentist
> * for medical reasons"

*(Additionally, the overview summary states you can visit "for tourism, business, study (courses up to 6 months) and other permitted activities.")*

---

### 3. Explicit Quotation of Prohibited Activities ("You cannot:")

Under the heading **"What you can and cannot do (‘permitted activities’)"**, the page explicitly quotes under **"You cannot:"**:

* *"do paid or unpaid work for a UK company or as a self-employed person, unless you’re doing a permitted paid engagement or event"*
* *"claim public funds (benefits)"*
* *"live in the UK for long periods of time through frequent or successive visits"*
* *"marry or register a civil partnership, or give notice of marriage or civil partnership - you’ll need to apply for a Marriage Visitor visa"*

---

### 4. Key Eligibility Requirements an Applicant Must Prove

Under the section **"Check you meet the eligibility requirements"**, an applicant must meet the following requirements:

* **Passport/Travel Document**:
  * *"You must have a passport or travel document to enter the UK. It should be valid for the whole of your stay."*
* **What you must be able to show**:
  * *"you’ll leave the UK at the end of your visit"*
  * *"you’re able to support yourself and your dependants during your trip (or have funding from someone else to support you)"*
  * *"you’re able to pay for your return or onward journey (or have funding from someone else to pay for the journey)"*
  * *"you’ll not live in the UK for extended periods through frequent or successive visits, or make the UK your main home"*
* **Extra eligibility requirements**:
  The page specifies that you may need to meet extra eligibility requirements if you are visiting the UK:
  * *"to study, do a placement or take an exam"*
  * *"as an academic, senior doctor or dentist"*
  * *"for an Erasmus+ placement"*
  * *"for a permitted paid engagement"*
  * *"for medical reasons"*
