## 2. Audit Protocol per Information Category

For each category, determine the **Evidence Surface**:
- `TEXT`: Visible, selectable rendered text in title, details, or specification fields.
- `IMAGE_TEXT`: Text or diagram embedded inside a product image or banner.
- `VISUAL_ONLY`: A physical product feature visible in a photograph without text labeling.
- `DYNAMIC`: An option or price that requires interacting with a selector to reveal.
- `ABSENT`: Not stated or displayed anywhere in the inspected scope.

## 3. High-Level Surface Summary

1. Which critical product facts were located **strictly inside image graphics** rather than text?
2. Which fields were **completely absent** from the page despite being relevant to a shopper (e.g., actual inventory quantity)?
3. What internal contradictions appeared between different sections of the same page (e.g., brand header vs description, or title vs photos)?
