## Method

For each question below, inspect the **original saved PDF only**. Record:

1. **Answer as represented by the website:** quote the shortest relevant sentence or describe the exact image/UI region.
2. **Location:** PDF page number and approximate top/middle/bottom or region.
3. **Evidence level:** choose `explicit words`, `directly visible image/UI`, `inferred`, `contradictory`, `not found`, or `cannot evaluate from PDF`. Multiple values allowed.
4. **Scope:** entire listing / selected variant / other variant / unclear.
5. **Does this verify real-world truth beyond seller claims?** Usually **No** for physical compatibility, inventory, shipped package, stylus/charging claims.
6. **Uncertainty:** record alternative explanations and exactly what evidence would resolve them.

Don't force Yes/No when the site doesn't supply enough evidence. “Not found in these pages” does **not** mean “doesn't exist” or “doesn't work.”
